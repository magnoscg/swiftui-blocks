With the [skill installed](/install/), ask for a piece of interface the way you
would describe it to a person. Your agent searches the catalog, picks a
component and says which one it chose before adding it.

## Tips

- Describe what it does, not what it is called: *a button you hold to confirm*
  finds `HoldToConfirmButton`.
- Spanish works too. Every component has keywords in both languages.
- Say where it goes, like *in the settings screen*, and your agent wires it
  there after adding the file.
- If your app supports an older iOS, say so. The agent checks the minimum iOS
  of a component against your deployment target and tells you when it needs
  a newer one.
